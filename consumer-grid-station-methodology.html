When a consumer grid station is required

A dedicated grid connection for major consumers.

The consumer grid station route applies where the sanctioned load, tariff category and utility interconnection requirement call for dedicated high-voltage infrastructure.

The methodology addresses industrial consumers under Tariff B4, commercial developments and malls under Tariff A2c, and housing societies under Tariff H, with the final scheme governed by the applicable DISCO requirements and approved load allocation.

Seven-phase methodology

A controlled path from concept to service connection.

Every phase closes defined technical, regulatory, commercial and construction interfaces before the project advances to the next critical milestone.

  1. 01
    Preliminary design · budgeting · pre-construction

    Project initiation & feasibility structuring

    Define the load objective, boundaries, site constraints, delivery model and information required to convert the requirement into a technically and commercially structured project.

    • Load data, utility interface and scope definition
    • Topography, site selection, contours and route profiling
    • Preliminary layout, budget, schedule and enabling works
  2. 02
    Technical clearance · load authorization

    Regulatory alignment & load authorization

    Coordinate the application route, approved load, point of interconnection, service-connection requirements and authority submissions so that engineering proceeds against an agreed utility scheme.

    • DISCO planning, design and operations coordination
    • Load allocation and interconnection-scheme alignment
    • Required environmental, inspector and statutory clearances
  3. 03
    Engineering design · equipment procurement

    Detailed engineering & strategic procurement

    Translate the approved scheme into coordinated primary, secondary, civil, control and protection designs, then procure compliant long-lead equipment against controlled technical specifications.

    • SLD, layouts, foundations, earthing, protection and control
    • Specifications, BOQ, bid evaluation and deviation control
    • Vendor drawings, type tests, manufacturing and FAT planning
  4. 04
    Construction · installation

    Infrastructure development & system installation

    Execute the civil, structural, line and electrical works through an integrated programme with disciplined HSE, QA/QC, interface management and progress control.

    • Site development, control building, foundations and trenches
    • Transmission interconnection, earthing and steel structures
    • Equipment erection, cabling, panels and auxiliary systems
  5. 05
    FAT · SAT · performance validation

    System verification & performance validation

    Confirm that every asset, interface and protection function meets the approved drawings, specifications, utility requirements and safe-energization criteria.

    • FAT and type-test review, inspections and site testing
    • Protection, metering, control, SCADA and interlock checks
    • Calibrated records and ready-for-energization recommendation
  6. 06
    Commissioning · staged loading

    System energization & operational commissioning

    Prepare and control the switching sequence, energize the installation in approved stages and demonstrate stable operation in coordination with the utility and the client.

    • Service-connection order and energization readiness
    • Switching plans, staged loading, monitoring and logs
    • On-load commissioning and operational endorsement
  7. 07
    Handover · compliance · O&M

    Project closure, compliance & lifecycle support

    Close the project with a complete technical record, trained operating personnel and a structured path for warranty, defects-liability and long-term asset support.

    • As-built drawings, test dossiers and authority correspondence
    • O&M manuals, training, spares, tools and software handover
    • Punch closure, warranty records and lifecycle support
